MY COMMENTARY ON SOME VERSES IN GENESIS
As an anecdote that sets the stage, I will relate a personal experience. I have a friend who happens to be a sincere Catholic, and I was trying to understand the difference between things that are the authentic direct word of God and things that were written by humans (and that might or might not be simply narratives to achieve a certain agenda, perhaps piggybacking on the authentic direct word of God). This seemed like a simple enough request on my part, in my ongoing effort to get authentic information, in order to avoid mistakes or bias. Surprisingly, this turned out to be a frustrating experience for both of us, as she was not able to provide even one authentic direct word of God, while at the same time not really being willing to say that she could not do so. I wonder if there is really anything other than what was written by a human, but then attributed to being direct word of God?
This text of Genesis was written by humans. It is beautiful and inspiring poetry, in the form of a metaphor of our best hopes. In that sense, it is akin to the U.S. Constitution/Declaration of Independence, expressing not the literal reality but the metaphorical hopes. While it might be inspiring to emotionally resonate with the hopes expressed in these metaphors, it is a grave mistake to misinterpret those metaphorical hopes as expressing a reality. For Genesis and the U.S. Constitution/Declaration of Independence, the literal reality could not be more different than the metaphorical hopes. Both the Bible and the U.S. Constitution would have been clearer to us, and an enormous amount of debate and contention could have been avoided, if the verbs had been expressed in the subjunctive.
Genesis is a metaphor for overlay and invention of human brain: existence is barren (both the physical world in genesis and the emotional world in the human brain) and a miracle happens in order to populate it with something that is more pleasant to humans.
In the tabulation below, I have added some commentary to certain verses. It could not be clearer that the goal of this portion of Genesis is to create an artificial hierarchy in which God is ‘established’ as occupying the top, and Man is ‘elevated’ to near-equality with God, highly suggesting Woman is just slightly subordinate, and Man has dominion over everything else. These memes are deemed so important that they are repeated several times. The emphasis effected by the repetition could not make it clearer that the goal of this writing is to create an artificial hierarchy. It is a canonical example of self-serving boot-strapping of God in order prepare the way for the self-serving boot-strapping and elevation of man.
A good portion of some of my other chapters have been devoted to explaining why this compelling metaphorical view, albeit artificial, is psychologically essential for human survival. Since the neurological core was evolutionarily built into our genome, well before humans had developed the technology of written records, these beliefs greatly pre-dated the explicit enumeration of them in Genesis.
