MOTIVATION
This essay is comprised of two related topics: (1) Establishing the most likely (or at least a highly plausible) model of the nature of underlying reality, and (2) Three options for how each individual may relate to underlying reality.

MODELS OF THE NATURE OF UNDERLYING REALITY
Models for the Nature of Underlying Reality
Currently, the two contending models for the nature of underlying reality are (1) religious and (2) scientific reductionist. Well- intentioned people who might want to try to avoid conflict will sometimes say that the two views are compatible and can co-exist. But the two views are actually mutually-exclusive, and a substantial effort is required in order to decide on one or the other.
THREE OPTIONS FOR HOW EACH INDIVIDUAL HUMAN MAY RELATE TO UNDERLYING REALITY
Background
There are three options for how an individual may relate to underlying reality. Since there are billions of individuals and only three options, we can expect there to be three large groups of humans. Within each group, the individuals may be bonded together to a greater or lesser degree by their commonality of fundamental outlook.
There may very well be a competition, either friendly and constructive, or (more likely) antagonistic between various factions of each group. In the extreme situation, members of the “other” two groups may be seen as posing an existential threat.
However, this perception is an illusion, since, even in the extreme event of total extermination of one group or another, within a short time the extinct group would become re-populated, either
4
by shifting affiliations of already-existing people, or by the next generation of people. So, there is neither a risk of a group’s becoming extinct, nor is there a hope of eliminating one’s competitors.
Three Levels of Awareness
To be somewhat less abstract, the three options are distributed among three levels of awareness. These levels can be descriptively termed “unaware,” “mistaken,” and “aware.”
Individuals in the unaware group are, in fact, unaware. That is, they take existence at face value, literally unaware of the considerations discussed in this essay. Whatever illusion evolution has created that serves to ensure continued human existence ( i.e., survival), is wholly and unquestioningly embraced. This is the default level of awareness for humans, and encompasses the majority of individuals.
Individuals in the mistaken group have a small degree of awareness that there may be options for choosing an underlying model of existence, but this question is of no concern to them, it may seem like an annoyance or a waste of time, and they basically are in full accord with the people in the unaware group. These are really unaware people who, to their dismay and annoyance, might have been exposed to some aware individual challenging their beliefs.
Individuals in the aware group are largely self-motivated, and likely have spent most of their life trying to figure out an answer on their own rather than accepting an “institutionalized” “pat” answer. Such individuals may very well eventually conclude that
5
unambiguous knowledge of ultimate reality is not currently (and perhaps never will be) attainable by humans, so it is necessary to accept a degree of ambiguity [1]. This ambiguity may not be satisfying. But accepting it is almost certainly more productive than buying into “pat” answers that may superficially appear to be more satisfying, but that are almost certainly without merit.
Practical Considerations
Regardless of the level of awareness, any individual can be targeted by a misinformation campaign. However, someone with a higher level of awareness is likely to have more intellectual and emotional resources with which to avoid being victimized. History is rife with examples of successful demagogues whose misinformation, coupled with bullying, was able to hold sway over a large portion of the population. Most unfortunately, this trend is happening currently. This is an ongoing problem with periodic ebb and flow. As discussed in other chapters of this of this manuscript (for example the chapter entitled “INVENTION”), the demands of the evolution of a survivable human brain inevitably result in a core genome and neurobiology that are susceptible to flawed perceptions of external reality.
Epilog
Being in the unaware group would not have been too bad under certain circumstances. Those circumstances might have held a long time ago, but certainly not now.
For instance, if nearly everyone is in the unaware group, if the population is not too dense, if communication networks are not too global, if weapons are not too destructive, if the structure of society is such that bullies and demagogues do not hold sway.

Under those conditions, humans might have been able to enjoy a happy and secure existence, without an accurate understanding of underlying reality.
