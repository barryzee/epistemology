This [document] is dedicated to the scientists, working in the health sciences of the United States of America, who one day this week without warning lost their jobs en masse , resulting in the most devastating threat to the well-being of the people of the world. My heart goes out to them and their families in the immediate moment. The long-term consequence for all of us is beyond our comprehension.
