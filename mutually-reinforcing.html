MUTUALLY-REINFORCING FACTORS THAT MAKE IT NEXT TO IMPOSSIBLE TO BREAK THROUGH
If external reality was sufficiently stark that
evolution needed to fool our brain into
inventing overlays in order to survive as a
species, then evolution also needed to make sure
that we did not see through these overlays and
the mechanisms that generated them. The same
basic mechanisms that generated the overlays
are also the mechanisms that prevent us from
seeing through them. So those mechanisms do
double duty. If we could have seen through the
overlays, then they would not have continued
working, and our survival as a species would
have been threatened. They may have been
appropriate for our survival at one time, but we
are now embedded in a totally different infra-
structure than existed when the bulk of human
evolution was taking place, and so they are
highly inappropriate now. Our eventual
survival or extinction will depend on our ability
to shed these illusions.
