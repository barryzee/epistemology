DOES SUPERMAN FLY FAST ENOUGH TO EXPERIENCE RELATIVISTIC EFFECTS?
The speed of light is 671 million miles per hour [
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speed_of_light
]
The fastest bullets travel more than 2,600 feet per second. That's equivalent to over 1,800 miles per hour.
[
https://www.wonderopolis.org/wonder/how-fast-can-a-bullet-travel
]
Superman is said to be faster than a speeding bullet, i.e., > 2,600 feet per second. If Superman was much faster than this, then a speeding bullet would not be used for making the comparison. The standard for making the comparison would be something that was closer to Superman's speed. That is, you might say that a human adult is taller than a human child, but you would not say that a human adult is taller than a bacterial cell.
So, Superman's speed is nowhere near the speed required for relativistic effects to kick in. In particular, even flying a top speed, still he would age at the same rate as anyone else on Earth.
