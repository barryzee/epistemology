HISTORY OF ESTIMATES OF THE AGE OF THE EARTH
The numerical value of the age of the Earth is one key to understanding human religious thought. If the Earth is relatively young, then there would not have been enough time for humans to evolve. By the process of elimination, direct creation of humans, as told metaphorically in Genesis, would be the only viable explanation. It is not surprising that many religious fundamentalists (mistakenly) cling to an incorrect very young value for the age of the Earth [1].
The (incorrect) young value might come about from a combination of two sources:
• Biblical or religious teachings [1]
• Rationally-intentioned, but erroneous, scientific measurements prior to the early twentieth century
As shown in Table 1, the correct values emerged as the result of our evolving understanding of radioactive dating [2], [3].
