BELIEF BASED ON SOMETHING THAT IS NOT POTENTIALLY KNOWABLE
1 There are several ways that something may not be potentially knowable to humans:
• So far away that we cannot reach it even if we could travel at the speed of light
(see 5:18 in “97% of Galaxies Are Moving Faster Than Light, HOW IS THIS POSSIBLE?”
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cadNZJvfl7s
).
• A human brain invents an abstraction that is carefully constructed so as to not be testable,
such as the existence of god, or something like “there are non-material non-visible dwarfs
dressed in pink polka-dot pajamas floating in some region of outer space.” However silly
the invented idea, according to the rules of logic we must entertain that it might be true so
long as we cannot disprove it (that is why agnosticism is preferable to atheism ). Of course,
in a few moments one malicious person can create any number of such inventions that are false,
but that could never be proven to be false.
2 Potentially knowable to humans, but not currently knowable would be something like the
composition of Moon rocks, if someone asked in, say, 1900. By 2021, that question is both
potentially knowable and currently knowable (this statement is a bit redundant, as something
that is currently knowable is of necessity potentially knowable).
