WILDLIFE VIDEOS FOR SELF-EXPLORATION
Watching wildlife videos provides an exceptional opportunity for self- exploration. There is a constant stream of emotional responses that are saturated with overlays, particularly inventions and value judgements. I have learned an enormous amount by looking objectively at my own emotional reactions. No one is immune from experiencing these reactions, the best you can do is to recognize them when they occur, and understand how they can mislead you.
I do not want to influence you too much, and I do not want to interfere with your own personal process. I will just suggest a few to watch, and leave you to find others and to experience/understand your own reactions.
